## About

I work at the intersection of **systems architecture**, **monitoring, evaluation and learning (MEL)**, and **strategic advisory**. I help governments, development organizations, startups, SMEs, and innovation ecosystems use technology and evidence to solve complex challenges and make better decisions.

Over the past decade, I have designed participatory M&E systems, built offline-first digital public infrastructure, and contributed to multi-country work spanning **climate change, disaster risk management, clean energy, and public health**.

My approach is grounded in the realities of implementation: unreliable connectivity, constrained budgets, diverse stakeholders, and the need for evidence people can act on.

> *A system is measured not only by what it can do under ideal conditions, but by what it delivers when the field demands answers.*

## Selected work

### 🌊 [HydroNexus One](https://hydronexus-one.netlify.app/)
**Digital public infrastructure for transboundary water management and AMR surveillance across SADC.**

- **Challenge:** Support water and AMR monitoring with a system suited to field operations and low-connectivity environments.
- **Built with:** React 18 · Node.js · MongoDB · Leaflet GIS · Arcjet
- **Reported outcome:** Trained 36 field operators and achieved 95% error-free data completion.
- **Focus:** Offline-first architecture · GIS · early warning systems · water governance

[Live demo](https://hydronexus-one.netlify.app/) · [Source code](https://github.com/Andre-tech671/hydronexus-one)

### 💼 Agritech investment readiness
**Financial and operational diagnostic for an agritech firm's $1.2M debt facility.**

- Identified a 0.38x debt service coverage ratio and subscriber-data verification gaps.
- Designed a seven-step reconciliation framework across 2,600 engagements.
- Developed a field-to-lab sensor validation protocol to strengthen operational and investor transparency.
