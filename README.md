## 🌍 Bridging the Gap Between Fundable Strategy and Field-Proof Execution

</div>

I am a **Technology, Data & Development Consultant** working at the intersection of three disciplines that are usually kept apart: **systems architecture**, **monitoring, evaluation & learning (MEL)**, and **strategic advisory**. 

My work focuses on helping governments, development organizations, startups, SMEs, and innovation ecosystems use technology and data to solve complex challenges, improve decision-making, and create measurable impact. Over the last decade, I have designed participatory M&E systems, built offline-first digital public infrastructure, and delivered multi-country portfolios across **climate change, disaster risk management, clean energy, and public health** in Southern & East Africa.

> *"The measure of a system is not what it can do in perfect conditions — but what it delivers when connectivity fails, budgets tighten, and the field demands answers."*
