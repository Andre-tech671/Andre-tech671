## 🌍 Building robust digital systems for data-driven public good

</div>

I'm an independent **MEL Specialist**, **Systems Architect** and **Senior Data Analyst** working at the intersection of three disciplines that are usually kept apart: **strategy & portfolio management**, **monitoring, evaluation & learning**, and **full-stack systems engineering**. Bringing all three to a single programme means **strategy that is fundable**, **evidence that is usable**, and **systems that hold up in low-bandwidth field conditions**.

Over the last decade I've designed participatory M&E systems, built offline-first digital infrastructure, and delivered **multi-country development portfolios** across **climate change, disaster risk management, clean energy and public health** in Southern & East Africa.

> *"I don't just describe the systems I build — this profile is one of them."*
